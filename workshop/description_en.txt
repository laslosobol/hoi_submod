[h1]Equestria at War: Expanded Stories[/h1]

[h2]IMPORTANT: AI-ASSISTED RESEARCH PROJECT[/h2]
[b]This mod is created with substantial AI assistance as a personal project exploring the capabilities and limitations of AI in game modding.[/b] Its code, narrative writing, localization and artwork are developed using AI tools, with direction, review and playtesting by the author. This is an experimental alpha: bugs, balance issues and awkward passages can remain. Please judge the result critically and help us improve it.

[h2]More stories for the countries of EaW[/h2]
Expanded Stories is an unofficial content expansion for Equestria at War. Its aim is to give countries more to do and more reasons to care about the outcome: new and expanded national focus trees, political paths, events, decisions, national spirits, and military and economic development.

The project is not limited to one country. The current release concentrates on [b]Cyanolisia[/b]; additional countries are a future goal, not content already included in this version.

[h2]Currently available[/h2]
[list]
[*]Expanded Cyanolisian paths involving the Countess, Ferdinand Dawnclaw, the Frontier March and the Imperial Administration.
[*]Reworked military and economic development, regional campaigns and post-war settlements.
[*]Grover VI's upbringing, succession and later rule, with different outcomes depending on the chosen path and his fate.
[*]Connected narrative events, lasting commitments, national programmes and custom portraits and icons.
[*]English and Russian in-game localization.
[/list]

[h2]Requirements and compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1826643372]Equestria at War[/url] is required. Enable it together with this submod, with EaW above Expanded Stories in the playset.
[*]Start a new campaign. Compatibility with old saves is not guaranteed.
[*]Other submods editing the same Cyanolisian or imperial files may conflict. For bug reproduction, test with only EaW and Expanded Stories enabled.
[/list]
