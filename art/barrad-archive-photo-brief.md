## Access and Editorial Rules

- Twelve attachments, not all eighteen cases. Other dossiers do not show an empty image slot.
- Field images and copied drawings require investigated or studied case flags.
- Crown, Chalice and Dark Heart secured-specimen photographs require a completed study.
- Attachments remain historical records after access/custody is lost; they never grant access or rewards.
- The amulet is a provisional survey drawing, not evidence of confiscation.
- The Titan image is a recovered joint drawing, not a claim of possessing a whole Titan.
- No imagery of captured rulers, invented bodies, or a revealed underground entity.
- Four images use selective redaction: rock plan, underground opening, reactor identifiers, Dark Heart internal detail.
- EN/RU captions are localized outside the pixels. Documents and exact Results remain separate.

## Shared Prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: an in-universe archival photographic attachment for a secret anomaly-research service in the Equestria at War fantasy world, circa interwar 1930s technology. Create ONE finished wide landscape image, aspect ratio 2:1, designed to remain legible displayed at 600x300. Silver-gelatin monochrome documentary photograph (unless scientific drawing explicitly requested), subtle fine grain, crisp midtone evidence detail, realistic worn materials and restrained forensic lighting, no heavily crushed blacks, no extreme vignette. Full-bleed rectangular photograph, no outer paper frame, no collage, no inset panels. No words, lettering, captions, numbers, watermarks, logos or heraldry anywhere; scale marks without numerals permitted. No humans, human hands, fingers or human anatomy; no people or creatures at all unless explicitly requested. World has ponies and griffons, not humans. No fleur-de-lis, SCP logo, modern electronics, neon UI, grotesque gore. This is fictional archival evidence, NOT a focus icon, poster or polished product advertisement. Important focal evidence must occupy central 80% and remain recognizable at thumbnail size. Redaction bars only where the individual prompt specifically requests them.
