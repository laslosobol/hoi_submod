# Secondary paths: focus icon prompts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool; transparent PNG originals, 99x86 TGA game exports. This batch replaces provisional sprite reuse for eleven March and Imperial Administration focuses. No gameplay changes.

## Shared direction

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one national focus icon for the Cyanolisia submod of Hearts of Iron IV / Equestria at War. A single richly painted dimensional emblem, antique gold edge highlights, blue-black steel, ivory parchment, cobalt-blue cloth, restrained crimson accents. Hand-painted strategy-game artwork, realistic material weight, crisp silhouette, not flat vector, not photoreal product photography. Isolated on genuinely transparent alpha, no backdrop, floor, cast shadow outside object or framing plaque. Compact composition fills 90% of square canvas, slightly wider than tall, all parts fully visible. One dominant subject and at most two supporting motifs, readable when reduced to 99x86 pixels. No text, letters, pseudo-writing, human body parts, hands, portraits, decorative random crests, fleur-de-lis, generic laurel wreath or watermark. Documents must contain meaningful non-text schematic marks such as ruled accounts, district maps, routes or simple geometric seals, never be empty. No national flags unless specifically requested.
