Inputs
------
The PSD's existing composite was exported without drawing new geography.
Verenia reference crop: (1450, 2010, 2310, 2720), size 860x710.
Cyanolisia reference crop: (2440, 2600, 3300, 3310), size 860x710.
Each country highlight was edited with the built-in image generation tool.
The results were visually checked against the source atlas; they are artwork,
not a live or pixel-exact representation of the province database.
No CLI or API image generator was used.

Icon prompt
-----------
Create a single transparent-background painted UI icon for the Equestria at
War: Expanded Stories country-selection tab, designed to be legible at only
78 by 70 pixels. An open ivory-paged chronicle with a deep emerald leather
binding, a few restrained antique-gold corners, and one pale feather quill
diagonally across its right side. Three-quarter near-front view, broad strong
silhouette, chunky readable forms, rich hand-painted grand-strategy game asset
rendering, crisply separated edges and small warm highlights. The book must
dominate the composition. One small red ribbon bookmark, no extra props.
Match the tactile painted gold-and-stone material feel of Hearts of Iron IV
fantasy mod UI, not a flat vector symbol, not a modern app logo, not a
photorealistic photograph. Centered, fills most of a nearly square canvas,
transparent empty margin on all sides. No circular badge, no shield behind
it, no rectangular plaque, no lettering or numerals, no wordmarks, no
pseudo-writing, no glow cloud, no floor or background.

Map prompts
-----------
Shared direction: Edit the existing EaW atlas crop with a strictly minimal
country-selection color treatment. Preserve every border, all city/country
text, rivers, railways, symbols, positions, framing and proportions. Do not
redraw the map, invent geography, add labels, translate text or add decorations.
Only the selected country's existing territory should brighten; its lettering
should be ivory. Keep terrain and paper texture visible. Neighbours remain
separate, readable, softly dimmed/desaturated. No frame, UI or caption bar;
preserve the source aspect ratio and the appearance of the original atlas.

CYA: Highlight COUNTY OF CYANOLISIA, the long coastal mainland strip running
diagonally from lower-left to upper-right through the centre, in muted
sapphire-blue with a softly luminous blue border. Do not highlight Asterion
or its islands. Preserve surrounding Brodfeld, Gryphia, Blackhollow, Sicameon.

ERI: Highlight HOUSE OF ERIE around Erie, Laile and Nidheim, below Avian and
west of Eyrie, in muted blue-grey/teal with a pale-blue border. Avian and Eyrie
remain unselected. Keep all roads, city lettering and atlas texture visible.

EYR: Highlight HOUSE OF EYRIE around Eyrie and Calembourg, north of Lake
Rumare between Erie and Angriver, in warm golden-olive with a gold border.
Avian, Erie and Angriver remain unselected and separate.

AVI: Highlight HOUSE OF AVIAN around its existing label and Mittelkatl,
below Greifenmarschen, west of Yale and north of Erie, in pale sage/sea-green
with a subtle silver-green border. Restrained printed-atlas highlight, no
harsh neon. Erie and Eyrie remain unselected and separate.
