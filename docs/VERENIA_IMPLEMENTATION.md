# Verenia: The Three House Campaigns

## Scope

Implemented on `master` as an unreleased Verenia package. It adds selected ERI and EYR focus trees, an AVI preserved-union branch, reciprocal events, shared diplomatic institutions, Erie's optional postwar Griffenheim reconstruction programme and two distinct Avian continuations. Existing icons and event art are reused; no graphics were generated or changed. CYA, the parked Barrad branch and the planning workbook are untouched.

Content includes both ducal diplomatic/military campaigns, domestic development, standalone Avian relations, cultural identity, a shared eight-focus institutional unification campaign and the separate imperial continuations. AVI retains sixteen regional constitutional focuses, twelve optional Herzland-settlement focuses and the seventeen-focus Zypressen continuation in its own tree. English and Russian localization is supplied.

Both ducal Houses have their own negotiated campaign and an alternative military settlement, without a new imperial succession for EYR. The regional settlement now leads to a common state, not perpetual independence for all three Houses. AVI's constitutional institutions are a preparatory phase. Its domestic and alternative political routes remain available, with the native Winterfest crisis separated from the constitutional choice. The limited regency still requires a genuine defensive Herzland settlement and consent; the Zypressen campaign belongs exclusively to the native fascist route.

## Institutional Unification Revision

The common campaign occupies x24/y2..9 for the duchies and x58/y2..9 for Avian. A convention establishes which House leads. Customs, common works, administration and military command have their own costs, benefits and implementation intervals before the constitutional vote. Final consent permits actual annexation and troop transfer. Only the six original Verenian homeland states become cores: EYR 391/400, ERI 401/500/673, AVI 470. Foreign conquests do not become cores. A military settlement must retain recorded victory and legitimate ownership; it cannot manufacture a peaceful signature or an imperial defensive victory.

Avian relations are detached at x42..46/y0..3. The actual `intergigantes` and `pursue_true_neutrality` focuses select the approaches; `proclamation_of_neutrality` is common to both and selects neither. The old Avian nodes inside the military narrative now concern domestic civil registers. A diplomatic ducal settlement can still conquer neutral Avian. An Intergigantes association can join the common convention, or accede later through customs alignment, an explicit accession vote, a 60-day transition and a final focus. Human refusal preserves the country; unattended accession events default to refusal, not surrender.

Cultural branches at x62..66 are independent of the settlement route: Erie can choose an Aquileian or bilingual programme, Eyrie a Herzland or bilingual programme. Neither receives the opposite foreign orientation. Research, literacy and educational institutions support these choices without another numerical meter.

Unified-state proof is carried into the optional Erie Griffenheim and Avian regency content. Annexed constituent tags are not treated as living foreign partners. These continuations still require their own original defensive-war and custody evidence.

An AI House waits for a human House's initiative. This also protects independent human Avian before charter membership, while its longer constitutional campaign is still being prepared. Temporary occupation does not waive that protection. An explicit neutrality/crisis choice or replacement of the constitutional government does not reserve a unification presidency for that unrelated Avian route.

## Route Revision (2026-10-23)

The common ducal opening leads to two mutually exclusive roots: negotiated settlement at x14 and military settlement at x34. Domestic development remains independent. The eight Griffenheim focuses now occupy x52..56, separate from Avian relations and cultural policy.

Diplomacy follows preparation, reciprocal guarantees, armistice, reopened roads, friendship and common institutions. Completing the armistice/treaty focus with an eligible AI counterpart performs the agreement directly; no random refusal, cooldown or foreign delayed response stands between completion and its reward. A human counterpart retains an explicit response. Guarantee preparations include a local scene choosing joint inspection or a 50-PP restitution fund. This is a one-time policy choice, not a new meter. A route-specific ceasefire lasts until treaty or actual renewed war, not a deadline that can strand an already-completed focus. Legacy native temporary ceasefires retain their 365-day duration.

The repeated ducal armistice, renewal, treaty, charter and Avian invitation decisions are removed. Genuine domestic consequences, optional prisoner exchange and compatible military coordination remain. If another human refuses, or the counterpart ceases to qualify, a reachable unilateral development ending preserves the diplomatic commitment without inventing an agreement. Switching from a chosen peaceful route to its mutually exclusive conquest route is not offered as a retry mechanism.

Already-completed old signing focuses receive one guarded recovery against an eligible AI, without granting unfinished focus rewards or overriding human refusal. Old saves that completed both former alternatives cannot be made into a clean mutually-exclusive campaign without rewriting player history; a new campaign is the reliable layout/route validation case.

The latest inspected game log showed offers six days apart despite a 90-day cooldown and no delivery entries. Block-form timed flags omitted `value`; explicit `value = 1` is now required throughout the package, with a structural regression check. This is a concrete format correction and a strong explanation for the missing state, not a claim that an in-engine A/B reproduction was performed. Production-script tests are not an engine playtest.

## Forceful Alternatives

The war branches extend the original feud, not a permanent obligation to conquer. Selecting the military root closes the diplomatic route and invalidates outstanding mediation offers. An accepted armistice, treaty, non-aggression pact or shared faction prevents a new hostile focus. War goals belong to focuses and never declare war automatically. Existing native decisions are preserved, not duplicated.

Recorded bilateral war, rival defeat and an actual peace conference precede a 30-day settling interval. All five original ducal provinces must then have the appropriate owner and full control. The rival can have ceased to exist or remain the victor's actual subject; neither case causes an automatic annexation, core grant or invented fate for its ruler. Occupation and third-party conquests do not substitute for victory.

Erie's arc revisits the promises made to its wartime supporters. Rosaline rejects her use as part of the spoils. Vincent chooses district rights or direct ducal government, then faces a supporter demanding an estate whose surviving title belongs to someone else. The judgement has a delayed consequence. The local-rights ending must bind the duke himself and can reverse his earlier exception; the centralised ending keeps a different institutional balance.

Eyrie's arc asks whether emergency command ends with victory. Iovinus chooses civilian release or lasting military tutelage. Montague's correspondence, reserve employment and workshop contracts test that decision. A later inspection returns to the actual policy choices, including unfunded civilian retraining or military production priorities. These are alternative obligations and benefits, not extra numerical meters.

Avian now has its own small branch, selected by its native political course rather than an optional pact inside the military narrative. Its conquest chain still requires recorded victory, a settled peace, control of the original homeland and a transition. A defeated actual subject must accept the articles before annexation; territory already annexed at conference receives a local-guarantees/direct-administration choice. Only state 470 becomes a core. Existing pacts and faction membership are not silently cancelled. See [forceful-settlement contract and source references](VERENIA_FORCE_CONTRACT.md) for the underlying victory and consent safeguards; the branch placement described above supersedes its former placement.

## Avian Crisis And Isolation

The regional integration is now implemented for all native routes without adding large technocrat or isolationist focus trees. The ball can follow valid Intergigantes negotiations or the legacy arrangement with two actual Avian subjects; it no longer requires the repurposed puppet-invitation focus. At its outbreak, each eligible House has seven days to keep its forces home, fund medical help, or support the lawful government. Avian's first native battle begins after fourteen days; subsequent battles retain native choices. Each House can add one point to the existing loyalist effort, once only. A newly possible highest-score tie is resolved for the aided loyalists, avoiding an empty winner event.

The military and technocrat victories retain native incorporation of actual subjects, but never take independent Houses or third-party subjects through a narrative announcement. Independent recipients may refuse supremacy, seek a civilian agreement, or expressly submit when live diplomatic conditions permit it. Loyalist victory remembers earlier assistance; later elections receive a separate response without changing the neighbouring government. True neutrality and Foundation admission have distinct correspondence and consequences. Foreign governments are not given Foundation secrets.

Civilian compacts need separate, current Avian acceptance of each House's proposal. They grant limited industrial/transit cooperation and a non-aggression pact, not the old charter, faction membership, territory or crowns. War, annexation, subordination or replacement of the relevant Avian government cancels the civil arrangement; occupation pauses benefits. The engine's normal rules govern any diplomatic non-aggression pact, including one that existed before these talks. Timed pending flags, deadlines, cooldowns and context checks prevent old replies from signing a later offer. Refusal and successful settlement both have delayed narrative endings.

Native `full_integration` now prepares the constitutional campaign instead of sending puppet invitations. Guarded legacy integration events remain for old saves, but cannot cross into the constitutional route. The native crisis remains a separate mutually exclusive choice. A genuinely renewed charter still restores completed institutional work without repeating its rewards.

See [crisis contract](VERENIA_CRISIS_CONTRACT.md) for exact ownership, sources and test boundaries. All agreed content families are implemented; this is not a claim that runtime playtesting, AI pacing or balance validation is complete.

## Erie Campaign

1. **The price of the feud.** Vincent hears public petitions or pursues private settlements. Rosaline and Montague's correspondence provides a personal opening without inventing a marriage or making them the dukes' children. Development and army work can proceed independently.
2. **Armistice.** Both preliminary focuses precede reciprocal guarantees. A local scene offers joint inspections (0.10 PP daily per House during the armistice) or a restitution fund (50 PP once). The next focus concludes the agreement with an eligible AI counterpart; a human retains the explicit offer. The diplomatic-route ceasefire does not expire while the player pursues domestic focuses. Acceptance stops native border skirmishes and removes mutual annexation/subjugation goals. Existing rivalry remains until the treaty.
3. **Treaty.** Reopened roads precede the longer treaty focus, which concludes friendship with eligible AI Eyrie without a second approval queue. The treaty ends the rivalry, makes the same cross-core concessions as native friendship and grants infrastructure/railway benefits, without annexation or faction membership. Human-to-human offers retain the original response scenes and valid-context guards.
4. **Common charter.** Erie can propose an equal council or a limited ducal presidency. AI Eyrie accepts equality or demands it in reply to the presidency; human Eyrie can also refuse. Civic representation changes the equal-council institutional spirit. Avian first decides whether to observe, then separately whether to join. Its absence or refusal never blocks a bilateral settlement.
5. **The first judgement.** The common court rules against an estate promised by Vincent to his own supporter. Compliance costs 75 PP. A ducal exception strengthens immediate authority but suspends all charter benefits in the signatory Houses. The treaty survives; a 100 PP decision can withdraw the exception and restore the institutions.
6. **Settlement.** Shared standards, joint planning and promised civilian works lead to the epilogue. A compatible AI partner joins the defensive league directly; a human retains a separate response, and third-party factions are respected. Rejection or loss of the counterpart opens the unilateral peaceful-development ending, not a switch to conquest or another invitation loop.

Remaining ducal decisions handle prisoner exchange, repairing a court breach and re-proposing a military pact when faction circumstances permit it. Core ceasefire/treaty/charter progression and Avian invitations no longer have repeat-request decisions. No numerical meters were added.

## Eyrie Campaign

1. **The cost of readiness.** Iovinus examines unreliable muster rolls and endless emergency requisitions. Public scrutiny costs 35 PP and improves mobilisation/stability at a continuing political cost; an army commission instead grants experience and training/organisation benefits, with a stability drawback. Montague's correspondence supplies a personal opening without forced marriage or invented kinship.
2. **Reciprocal restraint.** Eyrie has the same exclusive peaceful route and preparation sequence. AI Erie signs the completed armistice/treaty directly; human Erie retains the response events. Shared signatures and one-time policy flags make simultaneous focus completion idempotent. The legacy invitation register remains for human responses and native Avian mediation, not as a separate player progression loop.
3. **Civilian or professional accountability.** A settled frontier permits either civilian scrutiny, improving stability and supply consumption at a daily political cost, or defined professional command, improving planning and organisation. These are alternative lasting policies, not stacked rewards.
4. **Shared institutions.** Eyrie initially offers an equal council, accepted by AI Vincent; a human Vincent may request a limited presidency, which requires a separate Eyrie response. Avian has its own observer/membership decisions and is optional. Staff and workshop cooperation award meaningful research and experience alongside the conditional shared spirits.
5. **The return order.** Officers refuse to return a civilian workshop. Enforce the order for 75 PP, or retain military output at the cost of stability, civilian goods and all common charter benefits. A 100 PP recovery decision revokes Eyrie's exception. Erie's separate court breach is never excused by this recovery, nor vice versa.
6. **A dependable border.** Returned workshops yield one civilian factory in an owned, controlled core, or a construction-research fallback when no building site is available. A compatible AI Erie joins the defensive league directly; a human retains its choice. A newly founded league follows Eyrie's initiative; an existing consenting House-led faction keeps its leader. A failed conference has a unilateral peaceful ending.

New muster, inspection and requisition scenes are authored submod fiction. The canonical anchor is the existing feud between two ducal claimants, native friendship/integration content, Iovinus's military role and the existing Montague/Rosaline relationship. These new episodes are not presented as recovered EaW history.

## Avian Campaign

1. **Three signatures before one state.** After friendship on the Intergigantes route, Katrina opens constitutional negotiations. Native `full_integration` can begin this work as well; the HSM root is instead mutually exclusive with the second Winterfest. Late technocratic, loyalist, fascist and Foundation trees do not import it. Native AI political plans retain access to their separate crisis choice.
2. **Government in two languages.** A contested notice exposes unequal access to the administration. A funded translation office costs 50 PP and continuing political effort, improving stability and research; relying on local clerks instead improves stability and political organisation. Petitions from the towns supply construction expertise and distinguish domestic responsibilities from a claim to rule the neighbours.
3. **A charter without submission.** Katrina sponsors the existing shared negotiations. Erie and Eyrie decide their own constitutional arrangements, and Avian separately ratifies membership. A refused charter permits a peaceful neighbours' ending and later talks; it is not an automatic military alliance or vassalisation.
4. **Accounts, roads and a limited staff.** Shared services and staff use the same conditional institutions as the ducal campaigns. Each other House separately accepts or declines the common budget. Acceptance costs that House 35 PP and grants industrial coordination with a political-cost drawback; refusal is free. Avian gains the budget benefit only when at least one partner contributes. Both refusals still permit the story to progress, with a distinct budget scene.
5. **The unauthorised levy.** Katrina's officers demand funds outside the agreed powers. Paying for compliance costs 75 PP. Allowing the exception grants 100 PP and military output, at the cost of stability, civilian goods and suspended charter benefits. A 100 PP decision can rescind it. This is a third independent cause of suspension: repairing Avian's breach does not forgive Vincent's court exception or Iovinus's requisitions.
6. **A mandate to unify.** Honour the exemptions, build civilian works and publish the minutes. This completes the first constitutional phase and opens the common unification convention with Avian as a potential leader. The three governments still exist at this point, but actual integration is now the next campaign rather than a prohibited outcome. It does not automatically grant an imperial crown or regency.

The four Avian decisions only reopen failed charter/budget negotiations, restore its declined membership application or rescind its own unlawful levy. There are no counters or parallel decision-based progression systems. The language, accounts and levy episodes are authored extensions of the native dual-cultural state and its neighbouring family relationship, not claims about pre-existing EaW events.

## Development

Erie and Eyrie each have 18 development/army focuses accessible without winning a war or obtaining Avian's consent. They use EaW's existing poverty, literacy, industrialisation and scientific-base helpers, with unlock flags for the improved science laws. Schools, workshops, veteran employment and technical education accompany research bonuses and industrial construction. Avian retains its native development branches alongside the new regional politics.

Two earned research slots are capped at five at completion. Applied research requires four civilian factories and electronic-mechanical engineering; the research council requires ten total factories, basic machine tools and construction I. Military work provides equipment/support research, army experience, professional officers and logistics. It does not require annexing Eyrie.

Eyrie's equivalent laboratory milestone requires six civilian factories and electronic-mechanical engineering; technical fellowships require ten total factories, basic machine tools and construction I. Starting from its installed history, the complete branch reaches `poverty2`, `pre_industrial_society2`, full literacy and `proper_science_base`. It provides two civilian factories, one military factory and up to two infrastructure levels confined to its starting states. Its two earned slots likewise cap at five; treaty success is not required.

## Agreements and Existing Content

- Actual inter-House war voids the bilateral treaty and charter. Occupation or loss of independence pauses their benefits while preserving the historical record.
- An already completed native friendship is adopted once, unless an actual treaty breach was recorded. Completing an offer focus never manufactures another government's consent.
- AVI's original invitation does not pull a country out of an existing faction. Treaty partners can keep equal status when offered vassalisation, without the legacy refusal punishment.
- Native AVI coup events may annex only its actual subjects, not independent treaty partners or another power's subjects. Independent Houses receive a response event.
- The old AVI second-ball/coup route still requires its native vassal prerequisites. The equal union does not silently unlock that route.
- Before unification, shared courts, services and staff depend on a live charter. After incorporation, earned institutions become domestic components of the unified spirit. Development rewards earned domestically are not removed when foreign diplomacy fails.
- Negotiation cooldowns are enforced inside the shared dispatch effects, including AVI mediation. All diplomatic events have a 13-day response timeout; retry windows exceed the old negotiation and timeout lifetimes. War cancels pending consent, including war entered through allies.
- The completed judgement focus leaves an unresolved case for the daily dispatcher. If occupation prevents delivery, its 30-day delivery guard expires and the scene is retried when the charter functions again. Resolving it records the outcome once; no repeated PP charge or judgement reward is possible.
- Eyrie's workshop-return case uses the same recoverable delivery pattern. Separate suspension causes prevent either country's compliance from silently forgiving the other's breach. Stale responses cannot charge the cost or award a policy twice.
- Eyrie's two early policy scenes also retain unresolved, completed-focus state and retry after liberation. Their 30-day delivery guards prevent duplicate popups while a response is outstanding.
- The negotiating register remains on ERI even when EYR initiates. It is implementation state, not Erie's constitutional authority over its partner. Equal status is preserved in every treaty and charter outcome.
- Peaceful league dissolution clears the old bilateral military acceptance without bypassing the retry cooldown. A new league still needs consent. Late Avian charter signatories receive a separate military invitation; refusal is respected for that league and skipped delivery can retry after temporary ineligibility.
- An already existing AVI-led league is recognised only when ERI and EYR actually share it. Neither the new Avian branch nor this recognition creates membership or moves a country from another faction.
- Avian's language, budget-review and levy scenes retry after temporary ineligibility. Each policy resolves once. Budget invitations have 90-day retry guards, 30-day deadline events and a 45-day pending-expiry fallback if annexation prevented event delivery; an old response cannot act on a new offer. War between any two Houses invalidates budget signatures, whereas occupation only pauses benefits. Replacement of Katrina or entry into a native post-ball regime ends the budget arrangement, except for the specifically recorded constitutional succession of the real Grover VI.
- Removing Avian's charter membership also removes its ability to suspend the surviving bilateral charter. While it remains a signatory, its own outstanding breach continues to matter.

## Imperial Continuation

The optional eight-focus branch requires a recorded attack by the original GRI on an actual signatory, a qualifying defensive victory, a peace conference and ERI ownership/control of Griffenheim. Neither mere occupation nor an offensive conquest qualifies. A finite civilian mandate develops reconstruction, archives, technical cooperation and public accounts, ending in a final year or an immediate civilian handover.

Grover's confirmed death, existing local presence, foreign host and unknown whereabouts receive different text. No event transfers him, invents a regency, grants a title or interferes with CYA custody. See [VERENIA_IMPERIAL.md](VERENIA_IMPERIAL.md) for the precise entry conditions and expiry/interruption behaviour.

## Avian: The Limited Settlement

After the preserved-union campaign, a recorded original-imperial attack, defensive victory and peace conference can open a twelve-focus Herzland continuation. Griffenheim must belong to Avian; all original Herzland core territories must be held by Avian, its subjects or the independent signatory Houses. A 45-day settling interval leaves native postwar events time to resolve. Mere occupation, an offensive conquest or an unrelated imperial defeat is insufficient.

Katrina first establishes Grover's circumstances and commissions civilian reconstruction. The original character can be recovered from an annexed GRI only under strict ownership and custody conditions, or transferred voluntarily by a consenting Erie/Eyrie host. No other foreign household is touched. The two Houses then independently accept or refuse a limited regency for a living local minor, or a civil compact. The latter has different text for confirmed death, adulthood and unavailable or unknown custody; it does not manufacture a monarch.

Technical schools, a petition hearing, reserved constitutional rights and a choice between joint logistics and territorial reserves give the settlement domestic substance. Grover appears in the school and petition scenes only when actually present as a child. Those experiences return in his majority event. The date ends the regency automatically, even if a focus is unfinished. A living local adult can become constitutional head of the composite Avian-Herzland state once the agreed conditions hold; Erie and Eyrie remain independent. Loss of custody, death or a different regime ends guardianship without a fictitious succession. There are no new continental claims or territorial transfers.

See [VERENIA_REGENCY_CONTRACT.md](VERENIA_REGENCY_CONTRACT.md) for exact predicates, rewards and lifecycle boundaries.

## Avian: The Zypressen Continuation

Seventeen focuses extend only the native fascist tree. The original `a_zypressen_on_the_imperial_throne` still grants a war goal, not victory. The new coronation requires settled ownership and control of the Herzland after that native focus. The household register distinguishes Grover's actual known circumstances without seizing or duplicating him.

An imperial congress negotiates with the actual dependent rulers installed by EaW's existing dynastic content. Common ministries and autonomous crown agreements need the recipient's consent; a counteroffer returns to Avian for approval. Neither bargain changes autonomy levels, cores, ownership or rulers. Industrial works, technical colleges and a dispute over mobilisation accompany this institutional programme. New dependent crowns can be invited later without rebuilding the entire branch.

The western, northern, southern and eastern campaigns unlock in sequence. They follow the owners of defined regional territories, exclude friendly countries and create war goals, never automatic declarations. Recovery decisions only revisit already unlocked theatres or failed negotiations. The final accounts and crown congress follow a settled continental campaign rather than the act of selecting its first focus.

It covers the founding/settled state, earned ERI/EYR development, cultural and military policies, Avian constitutional institutions, common courts/staff/services/budget, and the post-union common-country choices. Later native regime replacements are routed through the same API so they replace predecessors, not stack. Shared components are boolean membership, not summed per signatory. Actual Avian idea possession is read before constitutional, late peaceful or defeated-subject annexation. Existing host programmes are deduplicated. ## In-Game Checklist

Use a new campaign with only EaW and this submod. Engine loading, rendering, event scheduling and save/reload still require verification.

1. Start ERI and inspect domestic development, ducal diplomacy/conquest, common unification, separate Avian relations, Griffenheim and cultural-policy blocks. Check tooltips and EN/RU strings; domestic development must not require a treaty.
2. Select diplomacy as each House. Inspect reciprocal exclusivity and the preparation -> guarantees -> armistice -> roads -> treaty sequence.
