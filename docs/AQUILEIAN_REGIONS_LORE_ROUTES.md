# Aquileian regional routes: national leadership or national support

Research: 2026-11-01. **Proposed routing, not implemented behavior.** The user's
clarification controls this matrix: the question is whether each movement seeks
to lead Aquileia or supports an existing NATIONAL movement. Regional government
is its political base and proving ground, not a separate-state end goal. The
earlier sovereignty/refusal interpretation is superseded. The fifteen old
programme sequences now follow actual local camp victory and peace.

## Recommended matrix

**Recommended defaults: nine support an existing national movement; six can
develop a competing national programme.** These are design recommendations, not
fifteen native outcomes. Leadership can mean a cabinet, coalition or collective
federal government; it does not always mean a new monarch or personal presidency.
Support still includes provincial reform, bargaining and national political work.

**C** = established native text or script; **I** = plausible inference;
**A** = authored continuation. Every recommended route is a proposal, even where
its motivation is canonical. Sources below give exact IDs and installed lines.

Native opposition to centralisation is evidence of interests, not proof of a
desire to rule Aquileia or to leave it. In particular, Dennis's refusal, the
communes' rejection of presidents and the Chevaliers' Vinovian identity require
three different readings. No route below has independence as its prescribed goal.

## Small routing contract for the revision

1. Auto-open the preparation root from the actual active native support phase
   (S7), with guards against reopening after resolution. Five focuses, sabotage
   for a mismatched commitment and temporary support/opposition spirits are the
   new **authored design**, not facts established by these lore sources.
2. Resolve the actual regional camp first. A losing pledge cannot reinstall its
   defeated ruler. Friendly/hostile central relations follow the resulting camp
   and government's conduct, not the player's earlier pledge or broad ideology.
   Keep any finer club-influence resolution separate (S8).
3. All fifteen programme sequences are postwar: establish a credible provincial
   administration, settle its governing programme, then participate in national
   politics. Distinguish **support an existing movement** from **seek the national
   mandate for our programme**. Neither branch promises a separate state.
4. Use the nine support defaults and six leadership proposals above. A supported
   movement can negotiate policy without replacing its national leader. A new
   leadership project must win support beyond its home province; victory over
   the opposing camp is not automatically a mandate from all Aquileia. Federal
   programmes concern the organisation of Aquileia, not secession. For the
   Chevaliers, write the step from local attachment to reciprocal national
   responsibility explicitly. Dennis's native refusal is not that step.
5. Player handover is an **authored integration mechanism**: native annexation
   does not itself implement a safe playable continuation. Do not equate merely
   loading a JER tree on a regional tag with returning to JER: native tree
   selection and many decisions use JER/tag-specific conditions (focus file
   lines 4–10; `JER_decisions.txt` line 220). Preserve national campaign progress
   and the actual incumbent; do not replay the revolution or force an election.
